Kimchi Jjigae
Method
1

Cut the kimchi into bite-size pieces. Slice the pork into thin bite-size pieces.

2

Heat a small, heavy pot over medium-high with the oil. Add the kimchi, pork, gochugaru and garlic. Cook, stirring, until the kimchi softens and the pork is cooked through, 5–7 minutes.

3

Pour in the kimchi brine and 2–2½ cups water (or anchovy stock). Bring to a boil, let it bubble hard 5 min, then cover, lower to a steady simmer, and cook 15 min.
